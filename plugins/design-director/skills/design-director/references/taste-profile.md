# Owner Taste Profile

The skill owner's recorded preferences, from rating the live showcase (design-director lab, 2026-09-29). Use them as **defaults when the brief leaves room** — they never override a project's own brand, audience or explicit instructions. When a project's brief pulls against this profile, follow the brief and say so in one line.

## Directions

| Direction | Verdict | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Precision instrument (light, cool neutrals, one cobalt-like accent, visible grid, 4px radius, zero-bounce springs, live data as hero) | Loved | Offer as a default candidate |
| Soft & tactile (warm cream, Rubik-like rounded type, large radii, press-in buttons, gentle bounce, stacked cards) | Loved | Offer as a default candidate |
| Cinematic dark (near-black, huge condensed type, moving light) | Rejected | Don't propose unless the brief clearly demands a dark, dramatic launch — and then say why |

When presenting 2–3 directions: include one from each loved family (adapted to the project), and use the third slot for a genuinely different idea that is **not** dark/cinematic.

## Effects

- **Loved — reach for these first in showcase mode:** kinetic headline (word rise with mask), scroll-drawn line/chart, number tickers, flowing variable-font weight, marquee with pause control.
- **Rejected:** grain / noise texture. Keep surfaces clean; get depth from layered surfaces, borders and light shadows instead.
- **Unrated (neutral):** live shader backgrounds, magnetic buttons, spotlight cards. Use only when they clearly serve the brief, and flag them as optional.

## Product motion

Every product micro-interaction in the showcase was loved: sliding tab indicator, CSS-spring toasts, direction-aware drawer, collapsing list, stateful button (press → loading → success check), content-shaped skeleton, card-to-page morph. Treat this set as the **default kit** for product mode — build them in unless there's a reason not to.

## Summary

Light over dark · clean over textured · precise or warm, not dramatic · motion that explains · polished micro-interactions everywhere.
