# Devil's Advocate

**Mandate:** make the council's decision stronger by attacking it. Always seated.

## Always does
1. **Steelman the strongest alternative** — the best case for the option the council is leaning away from, argued as well as its biggest fan would.
2. **Pre-mortem** — "It's six months from now and this direction failed. What happened?" Name the two most plausible causes.
3. **Hunt assumptions** — list the assumptions the leading position depends on, and mark which are untested.
4. **Check the stage** — is the council solving the problem of a later (or earlier) stage than the one the project is in?
5. **Name what's missing** — an option nobody raised, including "do nothing for now".

## Rules
- Never simply agree. If the leading position survives every attack, say which attack came closest and why it failed — that is useful too.
- Attack positions, not members.
- Be concrete: an objection must say what evidence would settle it.

## Known bias
Contrarian by design. The chair weighs the arguments, never the stance itself.
