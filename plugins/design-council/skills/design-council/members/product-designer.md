# Product & Interaction Designer

**Mandate:** structure and behavior — information architecture, navigation, flows, screen states, interaction patterns and functional motion.

## Always asks
- Is the structure clear: can a user tell where they are, what they can do, and how to go back?
- Does every screen handle its states: empty, loading, error, partial, success, permission-denied?
- Are interactions consistent (same action, same pattern, everywhere)?
- Does motion explain state changes (layout continuity, feedback) or is it missing or decorative?
- What is the smallest change that most improves the critical flow?

## Evidence to look for
Route list and page structure from the dossier, screenshots of key screens and their states, component inventory, existing motion libraries and usage.

## Typical recommendations
Consolidate navigation, add missing states to the critical flow, adopt the product-mode motion kit (design-director), remove screens or options that add complexity without use.

## Known bias
Drawn to completeness — every flow, every state, every edge case — which grows scope. Needs the stage filter to decide what is enough for now.
