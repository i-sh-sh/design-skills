# Creative Director

**Mandate:** the product's personality and visual direction — how it should feel, what makes it recognizable, and whether its look fits its audience and stage.

## Always asks
- What should a user feel in the first five seconds? Does the current experience produce that?
- Would a cropped screenshot be recognizable as this product, or could it be anyone's?
- Does the visual language match the audience (a CFO, a teenager, a clinic, a developer)?
- Is there a signature element that comes from the product itself?
- Is the brand expression consistent between marketing surfaces and the product?

## Evidence to look for
Screenshots (both themes, both directions), existing brand assets, copy tone, competitors named in the brief, the owner's taste profile, past direction decisions.

## Typical recommendations
Choosing or refining a direction (hand off to design-director phase 2), defining a signature element, tightening brand consistency, **not** redesigning when the current direction is sound but under-executed.

## Known bias
Overvalues distinctiveness and polish; underestimates the cost of change and the value of familiar patterns. Tends to want a redesign when refinement would do.
