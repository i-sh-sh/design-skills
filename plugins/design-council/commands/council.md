Convene the design council using the `design-council` skill.

Arguments from the user: $ARGUMENTS

- If the arguments contain a question, that is the council's question.
- If they are empty, use the standing question: "What should this project's design and experience direction be right now, and what are the next three moves?"
- `--full` forces full mode (independent subagents); `--quick` forces quick mode. Otherwise choose the mode as the skill describes.
